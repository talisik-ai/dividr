5 Add your voice

Words that stick with your story.

Add titles, captions, or subtitles in seconds. They live on their own layer — change them without touching anything else.

  • ✓
    Click anywhere on the stage to add text
  • ✓
    Pick a style — bold, subtle, or in-between
  • ✓
    Move it, resize it, change the timing — it's always editable
6 Give it feeling

Layer in music & voice.

Drop in a song, add your voiceover, balance the volume. You'll see the sound as a shape — so you know exactly where the beats land.

  • ✓
    Multiple audio layers at once — music, voice, effects
  • ✓
    See the waveform so you can edit with your eyes
  • ✓
    Adjust volume per clip, or for the whole row
7 Clean it up

Silence the background noise.

Traffic, wind, a humming fan, a room with too much echo — Dividr can take them out in one click. Your voice stays clear. Everything else gets quiet.

  • ✓
    Works on any audio or video clip
  • ✓
    No sliders to tweak — just turn it on
  • ✓
    Great for phone recordings, webcam audio, outdoor shots
8 Let it speak

Turn speech into subtitles, automatically.

Dividr listens to your clip and writes the captions for you — in your language. Every single word is timed to the exact moment it's spoken.

  • ✓

9 Last step

Save it. Share it.

When you're happy, click export. Dividr turns your story into a finished video file, ready to post anywhere.

  • ✓
    Pick a quality — Dividr handles the rest
  • ✓
    Your project is saved automatically as you work
  • ✓
    Come back later — everything will be exactly where you left it
✓ You're ready

★ One more thing

Want a feature that
doesn't exist yet? Build it.

Dividr is fully open source — and you don't need to be a programmer to change it. With today's AI coding assistants, describing what you want is often enough to make it real.

you ▸add a button that exports just the audio
claude ✓ added it — check your export menu
you ▸make subtitles pop up word-by-word, karaoke style
claude ✓ done — try it on any text layer

This is called vibe coding — describing the software you want and letting an AI build it with you.

What happens next

The roadmap? Yours.

Dividr is a sandbox. Nobody owns where it goes next. Every weird idea, every missing button, every "what if it could…" — you get to answer it.

  • ✦
    Try a strange idea just to see what happens

  • ✦

Dividr isn't meant to be finished. It's meant to be a playground — a place to experiment with vibe coding, without needing permission from anyone.
